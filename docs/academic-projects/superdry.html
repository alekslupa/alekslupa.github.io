About the project

As part of the BAA1019 Business Strategy module, our group of six acted as a strategy consultancy, A & A Consultancy, tasked with producing a full strategic review of Superdry. The report examined how the brand captured value in its early years, why it declined through overexpansion and brand dilution, and what strategic interventions could restore its competitive position.

The analysis followed a structured consulting process: Orientation, Diagnostics, Intervention, and Evaluation, applying frameworks including PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces, SWOT, RBV/VRIO, and the Strategy Diamond to build a comprehensive picture of Superdry's strategic trade-offs.

Key findings

Core problem Brand dilution and a shift to a discount-driven volume model eroded Superdry's premium differentiation
Frameworks used PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces, SWOT, RBV/VRIO, Strategy Diamond, ERRC Grid
Recommendations Rebrand around durability and responsibility, shift to digital-first DTC model, restructure the organisation
My role Associate Director. Led analysis and synthesis sections, contributed to diagnostics and recommendations

Reflection

This was my first experience working in a structured consulting format. Applying real strategic frameworks to a live business case made the theory feel tangible and practical. The group dynamics were challenging at times, particularly around the presentation, but working through those difficulties strengthened both the final report and my ability to collaborate under pressure.

Strategic data visualisation

The radar chart below maps the competitive intensity of each of Porter’s Five Forces acting on Superdry, scored from 1 (low) to 5 (high) based on our group’s strategic diagnostics.

import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
import numpy as np

labels = ["Competitive\nRivalry", "Supplier\nPower", "Buyer\nPower", "Threat of\nSubstitution", "Threat of\nNew Entry"]
scores = [4, 3, 4, 4, 3]

N = len(labels)
angles = np.linspace(0, 2 * np.pi, N, endpoint=False)
scores_ext = scores + [scores[0]]
angles_ext = np.append(angles, angles[0])

fig, ax = plt.subplots(figsize=(6, 6), subplot_kw=dict(polar=True))
ax.plot(angles_ext, scores_ext, "o-", linewidth=2, color="#4a90d9")
ax.fill(angles_ext, scores_ext, alpha=0.2, color="#4a90d9")
ax.set_thetagrids(np.degrees(angles), labels, fontsize=9)
ax.set_ylim(0, 5)
ax.set_yticks([1, 2, 3, 4, 5])
ax.set_yticklabels(["1", "2", "3", "4", "5"], fontsize=7, color="gray")
ax.set_title("Porter's Five Forces — Superdry", size=12, pad=20, fontweight="bold")
ax.grid(color="gray", linestyle="--", alpha=0.5)
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

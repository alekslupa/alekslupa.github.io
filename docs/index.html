Hi, I'm Aleksander Lupa — welcome to my ePortfolio.

I'm a final-year Business Studies student at Dublin City University, specialising in Business Analytics and fluent in both English and Polish. Over the past few years I've been exploring how commercial thinking, data-driven analysis and real-world execution come together — from academic strategy projects to live freelance work and a mock Dragons' Den pitch.

What defines me outside the classroom is just as relevant: I've built and shipped live client websites, co-developed a real product pitched to a mock investment panel, and planning to compete as a bodybuilder. I hold myself to high standards across everything I do.

This portfolio brings together work that reflects both my academic development and personal ambitions — from data analysis and strategic frameworks to reflective writing, cross-cultural research and web design. Each piece represents a step in how I've learned to connect analytical rigour with practical execution and clear communication.
